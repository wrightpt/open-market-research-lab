A SHORT FIELD GUIDE

Make a claim
someone can check.

01

Write before you run.

Create a study with a hypothesis, acceptance criteria and limitations. Select bounded method settings. Saved studies are immutable; a revision becomes a linked fork.

02

Leave a complete trail.

Run the local development experiment or download the submission, run it with the benchmark CLI, and import the resulting report. A submitted report is unverified until replayed.

python3 -m market_lab backtest submissions/your-study.json \
  --output artifacts/your-study-report.json

Place the downloaded submission under submissions/ in this same checkout. Import the report into its original study. Exported evidence packages also include the original report.

03

Reproduce, then disagree.

Switch to the other local demo persona and replay. The service compares the entire report, not just its headline metrics. Mismatches retain both versions. Record your review and any conflicts separately.

04

Take the evidence with you.

Download a JSON evidence package with the study, both reports, hashes, review history and attempt records. The local journal retains every accepted mutation across restarts.

What a matched replay means

The same engine produced the same output from the same synthetic inputs. It does not establish independent review, unseen-data validity, market predictability or trading readiness. Demo personas are not real accounts.
